What Happens When You Call?

  1. A few quick questions

    We ask about the space, its size, and whether there’s old paint or cracking, so we know what to bring.

  2. We pick a time to visit

    Estimates are booked on weekdays, with Saturday visits by appointment. Most take 20 to 30 minutes.

  3. We inspect and measure

    The slab gets measured, cracks are noted and moisture is checked. You can ask anything while we’re there.

  4. You get a written quote

    The quote lists the prep, repairs and coating system. Take your time deciding — there’s no pressure to book on the spot.
