Garage Floor Epoxy

Garage floor epoxy is a flake coating system for the floor most homeowners use every day. The slab is ground, repaired, coated with a base layer, covered edge to edge in vinyl color flakes and sealed with a clear topcoat.

It solves the problems bare concrete brings with it: dust that tracks into the house, oil and brake fluid that soak in for good, and old paint that peels wherever the tires sit.

It suits anyone who parks inside, uses the garage as a gym or workshop, or wants the space to feel like part of the house. Left alone, stains set deeper each year and peeling paint keeps spreading.

Metallic Epoxy Floors

A metallic epoxy floor uses mica pigments suspended in clear resin. The pigment is moved by hand while the resin is still wet, which creates the swirls, depth and pearl-like shimmer.

It solves a design problem more than a durability one: a plain gray slab in a home gym, game room, showroom or office that should look finished and intentional.

It suits homeowners and businesses who want a statement floor indoors. Without a proper topcoat, though, metallic floors scratch easily, so every one we install is sealed with a hard clear coat.

Read more about metallic epoxy flooring. Call us today for metallic epoxy flooring.

Commercial & Industrial Epoxy

Commercial epoxy flooring is a thicker, high-build coating system for warehouses, auto shops, gyms, retail spaces and back-of-house areas. It can include joint filling, striping, anti-slip texture and chemical-resistant topcoats.

It solves dusting concrete, forklift wear, stained floors that fail a walk-through, and aisles nobody can see. A sealed floor is also far easier to sweep and scrub.

It suits business owners, property managers and tenants doing an improvement before move-in. Ignored, bare commercial concrete keeps wearing down under traffic and the repairs get bigger.

Read more about commercial epoxy flooring. Call us today for commercial epoxy flooring.

Patio & Outdoor Concrete Coatings

Patio coatings use polyaspartic resin instead of standard epoxy, because polyaspartic stays clear and color-stable in direct sun. A flake broadcast in sand or stone tones gives the surface texture and hides dust.

It solves faded or peeling deck paint, rust and fertilizer stains, and slabs that just look worn out next to a newer house or landscaping.

It suits back patios, front entries, walkways and side yards. Left uncoated, outdoor concrete keeps absorbing stains, and painted patios usually need repainting every season or two.

Read more about patio and outdoor concrete coatings. Call us today for patio and outdoor concrete coatings.

Concrete Grinding & Crack Repair

Diamond grinding and crack repair are part of every coating job, and they’re also available on their own. Grinding strips old paint, mastic and sealers and leaves the slab ready for a coating, tile or polished finish.

It suits homeowners prepping a floor for another trade, and anyone with a slab covered in failed coatings. On post-tension slabs, cracks are filled and ground flush rather than cut out, which keeps the tensioned cables safe.

Ask about grinding and repair at your estimate. Call us today for concrete grinding and crack repair.
